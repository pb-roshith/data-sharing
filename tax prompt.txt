You are a senior AI architect, knowledge graph engineer, Python developer and banking credit-risk domain specialist.
 
Build a working prototype called:
 
CREDIT POLICY INTELLIGENCE
Ontology-Driven Policy Rule Extraction and Knowledge Graph
 
The purpose of the application is to demonstrate how an enterprise risk schema, risk-domain reference documents and credit-policy documents can be transformed into:
 
1. A controlled Risk Taxonomy
2. A Risk / Credit Policy Ontology
3. A Policy Knowledge Graph
4. A structured Policy Rule Library
5. An explainable mechanism to determine which policy rules apply to a credit request
 
This is a demonstration/prototype, not a production policy decision engine.
 
===========================================================
1. HIGH-LEVEL BUSINESS PROBLEM
===========================================================
 
Banks have large numbers of policy, standard, procedure and guideline documents.
 
Credit officers need to determine:
 
- Which policies apply to a credit proposal?
- Which individual policy rules apply?
- What conditions and thresholds are relevant?
- What approvals are required?
- Are there exceptions?
- Are there conflicting or overlapping rules?
- Why was a particular rule considered applicable?
- Where exactly did the rule originate?
 
The prototype should convert unstructured policy documents into a structured and traceable Policy Knowledge Graph and use that graph to identify potentially applicable rules.
